Status: setup committed, test list written and reviewed by four outside readers. Nothing is implemented yet. Answer the three yellow boxes and I start building. Everything else is written down and you can skip it.

1. Three calls only you can make MUST DECIDE

A. Where does the discounted line live? Pricing (PO) and Billing (BL) do not exist yet. To decide "is ₹251 more than 10 % of this pitcher" the server needs the pitcher's list price from somewhere, and once approved it needs somewhere to write "₹251 off, reason placard, approved by Priya".

My default: a new small document per line, restaurants/{id}/lines/{lineId}, holding list price, whether it was sent to the kitchen, the discount or void, and a version number. Tests seed it; later PO/BL write it for real. Nothing existing changes.
The other way: write straight into today's order documents (orders → carts → items). Every Flutter app reads those, so that is a schema change to live data.
Answer with: "lines doc" or "orders".
B. Over the limit, whose PIN? The sheet says the cashier types their own PIN to approve their own ₹251 discount. That is a speed bump, not a control: a lone cashier at 11 pm can approve themselves. Every open-source POS we looked at makes over-limit something a cashier account simply cannot do.
My default: own PIN, as the sheet says. One config key later (approvals.requireDifferentApprover) flips it to "a manager's PIN", no schema change.
Answer with: "own PIN" or "manager's PIN".
C. Limit in percent only? 10 % of a ₹300 beer is ₹30. 10 % of a ₹40,000 banquet table is ₹4,000 with no PIN asked. Gemini flagged this; it is your open question PO-Q1.
My default: percent only in v1. A rupee cap is one more config key later.
Answer with: "percent" or "percent + rupee cap".

Two more from the sheet's own sign-off list, both marked fine to skip: the owner (ADMIN) is also asked for a PIN over the limit (default yes); captains cannot request approval from their phone, they walk to the till (default yes).

2. Calls I made while merging reviews FINE TO SKIP

Each one is a line in the sheet's Decisions table. Say so if any is wrong.

No account lock, a slowdown instead (sheet R7, evening edit). From the 5th wrong PIN in ten minutes the next try waits 1 s, then 2, 4, 8 … up to 2 min. The account keeps working for everything that needs no PIN. Owner gets one P0 row per streak.
Two cashiers discounting the same line: both apply, one after the other, two audit rows. Last one wins on the line. The audit trail shows both. No "second one fails" error.
A second discount replaces the first, it does not add (sheet R9). ₹100 then ₹251 means ₹251 off, not ₹351. Money on a line is menu price − offer − discount, in paise, never below zero. The Monday rupee column sums the lines, not the audit rows (R10).
Reprint now writes an audit row (P1). The donor review caught this: without a row, "print two bills, settle on the second, keep the cash" leaves no number in the Monday report.
"Ten minutes" became a config key. approvals.pinLockWindowMinutes, default 10. The sheet had it as a literal.
Kitchen strike-out on void is deferred. The kitchen app reads the old order schema; the "struck line" marker is already assigned to the OR module. ST writes void on the line; OR shows it.
Reducing quantity of a sent line is a void. 2 biryanis → 1 removes ₹450; when the till gets line editing it must go through this same door. No test until then.
Voiding an already-voided line is an error, not a silent OK. A double tap should be visible.

Reviewer ideas I rejected (one line each)
  • Backwards-clock test for the lock: tests use a fake clock; no code for a state the platform clock should make impossible.
  • Idempotency key on the request: not needed, see "replaces, does not add" above.
  • "Discount on a settled bill": no bill exists until BL ships; BL adds that check.
  • Audit row when the owner changes the limit: config editing is not in this module; noted for the Config sheet.
  • Cache the config in memory: the sheet says the next request uses the new limit; one read per risky action is the price.
  • Simpler lock counter (gap between failures): the sheet says five in ten minutes; a timestamp list is three lines and matches it.
  • Reversing kitchen ticket on void: belongs to OR and printing, not here.
