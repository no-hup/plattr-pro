## 3. Two decisions that are yours, not mine

**Legacy tax-shaped charges collide with tax blocks.** `res_salt` seeds `{ type: 'GST',
percentage: 5 }` inside `billing.charges` — tax done the old way. With a food tax block now
present, GST lands twice, and because charges are folded into their block's taxable base, that
GST charge is itself taxed. Observed on a real bill: taxable ₹372.90 = ₹330 net + ₹26.40 service
charge + ₹16.50 GST-as-a-charge, then CGST/SGST computed on all of it. `res_chowman` carries a
`LOYALTY_DISCOUNT -5%` in the same list. Either the seed drops tax-shaped charges wherever tax
blocks exist, or BL's sheet needs a written rule for what happens when both are configured.

**Order offers discount alcohol.** On a Meghana bill the ₹100 flat offer apportioned ₹41.83 onto
the beer line. Whether liquor may be discounted is a Karnataka excise question. If the answer is
no, the apportionment needs to skip non-collecting blocks and that is a BL rule, not a seed fix.

## 4. `test/e2e/journey.mjs` — who owns it

I wrote it as a reviewer sanity harness; it is still untracked. Someone extended it with a step 3b
for the kitchen and serve lifecycle and a TD-013 note (the table is vacated at `COMPLETED`, before
the bill is paid). Good addition, real find. Decide between you who owns the file before committing it.

Three corrections I made to it, so nobody re-fixes them:

- **Charges are inside the taxable base.** `payable === taxable + tax + roundOff`. Adding charges
  again separately double-counts. My first version had this wrong and it failed on `res_salt`.
- **It was wiping `billing.charges`.** Overwriting them with `[]` destroys the seed's real service
  charges and changes what the consumer app shows. Charges are kept by default now; `--no-charges`
  drops them deliberately.
- **`--as-seeded`** skips all stamping and bills the restaurant exactly as the seed left it. That is
  the flag that proves the seed is self-sufficient.

Usage, from `backend/src-plattr/functions`:

```
node test/e2e/journey.mjs res_meghana --as-seeded            # 43 checks, ~0.5s
node test/e2e/journey.mjs res_meghana --as-seeded --liquor=bar   # food + liquor on one bill
node test/e2e/journey.mjs res_meghana --setup-only           # park a draft, print the till URL
```

All five MockData7 restaurants pass 43/43 as seeded.

What it covers that the `billing` and `payments` e2e suites do not: those seed synthetic `lines/`
documents, so nothing until now proved a **real menu item ordered through the real cart** reaches
a bill and gets paid. This walks OTP → cart → checkout → line snapshots → kitchen → serve →
complete → preview → issue → part card → rest cash → settled, and re-derives every rupee
independently of the server's own arithmetic.
