# PY · Payments — build plan (draft for Shaurya, 2026-09-15)

Written while BL is being planned by the other session. PY is the module after BL. Nobody starts it
until BL's sheet is signed; this page exists so the handoff is a one-liner when that day comes.

**Job.** Take a finalised bill to paid: one or more tenders (cash, UPI, card, other), a payment
split, change given, a refund against a credit note, and a "paid" mark that nothing else can set.
Day close (DC) sums what PY recorded. Gateways (dynamic UPI QR, card terminal) are later modules
that call PY's `record`; PY v1 is the ledger, not the pipe.

**Why PY before OF (Offline & sync).** BL's cancel-versus-credit-note branch depends on "is it
paid", DC has nothing to count without PY, and OF's sync model should be designed over the full
money path (line → bill → payment) rather than half of it. PY is small, about half of ST.

## What exists today, and what is wrong with it
- `orders/orderConstants.js` has `PAYMENT_STATUS {unpaid, partially_paid, paid}` on the order doc.
  `partially_paid` is never written. `paid` is set by `order-updateOrderStatus` COMPLETED
  (`orders/updateOrderStatus.js:156`) with no tender, no amount, no who. Admin reads it with a
  fallback `'pending'` that is not in the enum (`adminApp/orders_admin.js:102`).
- COMPLETED rebuilds the price info without charges (pre-launch finding). PY does not fix that; it
  stops depending on it: paid is decided by PY's rows against BL's payable, never by order status.
- Served-but-unpaid orders vanish from the server app (lifecycle blocker 1). PY's till screen is
  the new route to "take payment", so the old Mark Paid path becomes legacy, not a fix target.
- No payment row exists anywhere. Nothing records cash vs UPI. Day close today is impossible.

## Calls I will make unless you say otherwise
- **Paid is derived, never set.** `bill.paidStatus` is written only by `payments-record` from the
  sum of its own rows against BL's payable. `order.paymentStatus` keeps working for the Flutter apps
  by being mirrored from it, one line, until those apps move to the till (TD to log).
- **Tenders are config.** `payments.tenders` list on the restaurant, default `[cash, upi, card, other]`,
  each with a label and `needsRef` (UPI and card want a reference string). Adding "Sodexo" is a row,
  not a release. No country switch, same as BL.
- **Overpayment is only legal in cash**, and the difference is change. UPI or card over the
  payable is refused; the row is not written.
- **Idempotency key from the till.** A double tap or an offline replay carries the same key and
  gets the same answer. This is the one seam OF needs from PY; designed for, not built.
- **Refund only against a credit note** (BL's reversing doc), never against a bill. Refund tender
  defaults to the original tender; cash refund of a UPI payment is allowed with a PIN (ST, P2).
- **Split by amount lives here; split by lines lives in BL.** Equal split puts the residual
  paisa on the last share, as in BL's tax parts.
- **Partial payment holds the table.** A bill with rows but not fully paid is `partial`; table
  stays occupied; DC will flag it at close.
- **Out of scope v1:** gateways (dynamic UPI QR, card terminal push), tips, wallets and loyalty
  credit, foreign currency, cheque, credit accounts (tabs), payment on a draft bill.

## Open questions (answer in chat; each changes the work)
1. **Rounding of the cash tender.** BL rounds the payable to the rupee (config). Should UPI and
   card pay the unrounded amount and cash the rounded one, as many Indian bills do? Default: one
   payable for all tenders, the rounded one. Simpler; the accountant may want the other.
2. **Who can refund.** Cashier with PIN over a config amount, or manager only? Default: PIN above
   `payments.refundPinAbove` (config, default ₹500), manager PIN like ST.
3. **UPI without a gateway.** Until the QR module exists, the cashier confirms "received" by eye
   from the shop's phone. Fine for the first customer, or do you want a "pending confirmation"
   state for UPI from day one? Default: no pending state; the row is the confirmation.
