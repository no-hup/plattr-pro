# Donor inventory — offline & sync (repeat submissions; what prints when the server is gone)

## Recognising a repeat
- Client mints a uuid per order, per line, per payment; the DB refuses a second one — `pos_order.py:369,388`, `:1659,1670`, `pos_payment.py:44,46`.
- Replay is looked up by uuid, newest first, before anything is written — `pos_order.py:1258-1259`.
- Repeat on a draft updates in place; repeat on a non-draft is ignored, the existing id is returned, logged, and the cashier sees success not an error — `pos_order.py:1296-1309`.
- A line re-sent as CREATE with a uuid already on the order is rewritten to UPDATE, so a replay cannot double a line — `pos_order.py:113-123`.
- The server recomputes `amount_paid` from the stored tender rows and never trusts the replayed figure — `pos_order.py:190-195`; tenders are immutable once posted — `pos_payment.py:60-70`.
- Every tender added, changed or removed is written to the order's own log with old and new method and amount — `pos_order.py:626-685`; a tender cannot change on an order already printed — `pos_order.py:349,623`.
- Dolibarr has no key at all, only state: validated with nothing left to pay answers "InvoiceIsAlreadyValidated", otherwise the amount is clamped to the remainder — `invoice.php:374-381`, `:417-433`, `:464-469`. **LIMIT: a repeat while a remainder exists books a second tender; `:188` reads the amount as a float off the request, `:425` takes change from the browser.**
- URY guards by stored state too: a printed/billed invoice refuses modification unless the actor holds billing rights — `ury_order.py:1314-1325`, `:1343-1348`.
- SambaPOS has no client key — a ticket is its DB `Id`, `Id == 0` means unsaved — `Ticket.cs:26-28`, `:502`. **LIMIT: a resubmitted unsaved ticket becomes a second ticket.** It makes settlement idempotent by arithmetic instead: `remaining = sum − payments + change`, close only at zero — `Ticket.cs:436-441`, `:80-86`, `:758-760`.

## What the kitchen and the guest see on a repeat
- The kitchen print is a diff against the stored snapshot, not a resend; removals go out as a cancel KOT — `ury_kot_generate.py:409-423,426-432`, `ury_order.py:1517-1525,1577`.
- Dolibarr flags each line printed (`special_code='4'`) and skips flagged lines next time — `invoice.php:1407-1410,1444`. **LIMIT: flagged after the send regardless of whether the printer answered.** SambaPOS locks a line once sent; locked lines can only be voided — `Ticket.cs:521-529`, `:487-490`.

## When the terminal cannot reach the server
- The receipt number is issued by the server at create, never by the till — `pos_order.py:589-592`, `pos_config.py:216-220`; the short guest-facing tracking number comes off the same sequence (`next % 1000`) — `pos_config.py:219`. Offline, no final number prints.
- Dolibarr's open bill carries a provisional ref `(PROV-POS<term>-<place>)` until validation replaces it — `invoice.php:222,796,1894` — and prints a non-fiscal "temp" ticket from it — `invoice.php:1724-1732`.
- The card terminal is start/status/cancel, cancel explicitly advisory because the terminal may already have captured; the default provider refuses honestly rather than faking approval — `payment_terminal.py:47-72`, `:74-90`.
- An order replayed into a closed session is moved to any open session on the same till, warning with session, uuid and total; no open session means refusal — `pos_order.py:31-53`, `:69-71`.
- Orphan orders land in an auto-created Recovery session excluded from the usual constraints — `pos_session.py:84-87`, `:317`. **LIMIT: closing a rescue session forces counted cash = theoretical, erasing the discrepancy — `pos_session.py:401-408`.**
- Write-replay queue: out of scope. Reserved offline number ranges: no donor has them.

## MISSING
- No per-line "already sent to the kitchen" flag: the sheet dedupes the whole checkout (OF-S1) but nothing marks a line printed, so any reprint or partial resend reprints the whole ticket — `ury_kot_generate.py:409-423`, `invoice.php:1407-1410`, `Ticket.cs:521-529`. 20:55, a ₹80 coke is added to table 7's 20:41 order and the kitchen cooks two more biryanis, ₹900 walked.
- Nothing says what happens to a retry that lands after the business date it belongs to is closed; Odoo moves the orphan into an open session and refuses when none is open — `pos_order.py:31-53`. 00:05, the guest app's ₹900 retry answers against a sealed 2026-09-16 and shows in neither day's cash.
- No in-doubt tender: cancel is advisory and a card/UPI may already be captured — `payment_terminal.py:74-90`. 21:05, ₹1,840 UPI, the guest's phone says failed, the bank captured it; the estimate records cash and the guest is charged twice.
- An estimate is not frozen once printed; Odoo refuses a tender change on a printed order — `pos_order.py:349,623`. 20:50 the cashier retypes 20:45's ₹2,310 estimate as ₹1,310 and reprints; the guest's copy and the till disagree by ₹1,000.
- Nothing refuses a reconcile that would exceed what is owed; Odoo refuses a refund past the outstanding qty and recomputes paid from the rows — `pos_order.py:1824-1831`, `:190-195`.

## DIFFERENT
- Donors put the key on the row itself with a DB unique constraint — `pos_order.py:388`, `pos_payment.py:46`; R1 keys an optional `requestId` on the cart snapshot and compares a content fingerprint, and OF-S3 keeps a no-id path. The constraint is safer for the money: it still holds when a new code path forgets the check.
- Odoo answers a repeat on a settled order with success and the existing id — `pos_order.py:1303-1309`; Dolibarr answers an error — `invoice.php:380-381`. The sheet's `retry: true` success is Odoo's, and is the right one for a lone cashier at 11pm, who reads any red box as "tap again".
- Odoo drops an orphan into a rescue session and then forces counted cash to match theory — `pos_session.py:401-408`; the sheet refuses day close until reconcile (R6) and lets OF-S9 read over by ₹2,310. The sheet is safer: the drawer's real gap survives to the morning instead of being papered over.
- Dolibarr silently clamps a tender to the remainder server-side — `invoice.php:431-433`; OF-S11 records the ₹630 actually taken and leaves ₹399 outstanding. The sheet is safer — a clamp hides the gap the cashier needs to see.

## OURS ALONE
- Recording an amount and a tender offline on a printed estimate, with nothing sent anywhere (OF-S7, R8, R4) — no donor writes money down while the server is unreachable.
- The till pushing every estimate into the audit trail by itself on the first answered call, before any human acts (OF-S20).
- Day close refusing to open while an estimate is unreconciled, gated on both sides (R6, OF-S10).
- An orphan estimate's cash booked as a named drawer cash-in with an `est:` ref when the draft is gone (OF-S19).
- A maximum age on the cached preview that refuses to print an emergency bill at all (OF-S12).
