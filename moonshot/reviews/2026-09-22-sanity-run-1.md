## Findings, in order of weight

1. **Bills never attach to their sitting on a real order (the BL/FL seam).** `app/floor.ts`
   `attach()` and the adapter's `getSitting()` match a bill to a sitting by the bill's
   `sessionId`. The real `billing-issue` writes the **staff login** into `sessionId` and
   the sitting into `draftId` (`app/billing.ts:127`; the document confirms it:
   `sessionId: LXtyKe…` = till@meg.test's session, `draftId: gxHhJb…` = the guest). So on a
   real bill the floor sees lines marked billed and **zero bills**. Every till spec seeds bills
   with the sitting's id in `sessionId`, which is why 23/23 floor and 2/2 billing were green.
   STATE.md's "bills are tied to a sitting by their own sessionId" is wrong in practice.
2. **The settled tile has no identity, and Clear 500s instead of refusing.** After payment
   the floor showed a tile whose testids were literally `tile-`, `settled-`, `clear-` (empty
   label, empty `tableIds`), while `tile-6` still existed with no amount. Clear sent an empty
   `tableId`; the backend threw Firestore's "documentPath must be a non-empty string" and
   surfaced "An unexpected error occurred". Should be `invalid-argument` naming the table.
   Same root as (1) plus: the bill was issued with `tableIds: []` — the bill screen is
   reached by `?draft=<session>` only and never learns the table.
3. **Till login is lost on every floor → bill hop.** `FloorScreen.tsx:45` navigates with
   `location.assign` (full reload) and the login lives in React state with nothing in
   storage. A cashier taps a table and gets the login form. The floor spec re-logs-in after
   every navigation (`floor.spec.ts:97,102,108,114`), which hid it. The driver re-entered
   credentials at hops 4, 5 and 6.
4. **Floor tile ₹260, bill ₹258.** The line snapshot carries `offer: null`; the order-level
   offer lives only on the order (`appliedOffer`). The tile is "net over unbilled lines", so it
   shows the pre-offer price. Related to TD-012.
5. **`line.sent` is already true at checkout** on the default (no waiter-confirmation) path,
   so hop 3's "sent flips" assertion cannot fail here. Fine for the sitting; the card's
   expectation was wrong.
6. **Kitchen card id is `kitchen-cart-<orderId>_<index>`**, server card id is
   `order-cart-<cartId>`. Two conventions for one object. FRONTEND_TESTING §6 says `<cartId>`
   for both.
7. **`ab login server meg` prints a browse timeout and "no login-submit" on a login that
   succeeded.** A driver trusting the exit text reports a broken login. The poll after
   submit needs a longer budget or a better "past the form" signal.
8. **`preview` on the bill screen produced no visible change**, since the screen already
   shows the preview on load. Not a bug; the card should not expect a diff.

Not bugs, resolved by the observer:

- The driver flagged "session activated with no OTP typed". The debug consumer build prefills
  `123456` from `config/dev_guest.dart`, and the emulator log shows `table-validateOTP` ran.
- `mi_veg_bir` opens a customization sheet (`isCustomizable: true`, a ₹0 "Single" portion).
  The card said it adds directly; the driver handled it with `sheet-add`.
- The line says `placedBy: system` for a guest order. Cosmetic, worth a look when the line
  schema is next touched.

## What the split proved

Two false alarms (OTP, customizable dish) and one misread of my own (I first called
`tableIds` non-empty) were caught only because two readers looked at the same documents. The
seam bug (1) was invisible to 39 unit suites, 21 e2e suites and 6 Playwright specs because
every one of them seeds the bill it then reads. One real order through the screens found it in
25 minutes.

## Fixed 2026-09-25 (findings 1 and 2)

Grok (grok-4.7) was consulted blind on the design first; both converged on "the bill reads its
sitting and tables off its own lines". Accepted from Grok: rename the field so the word `sessionId`
never again means the cashier on a bill; a stamped line whose bill the floor cannot read throws.
Rejected: "cancel may leave lines stamped" (it frees them), "keep the no-session fallback" (the
only caller refuses a checkout without a session).

- **Bill.** `sessionId` is gone from the bill. `sittingId` and `tableIds` are read off the bill's own
  lines inside the issue transaction; lines of two sittings, or a line with none, are refused. The
  till no longer sends `tableIds`. A credit note carries its parent's sitting; its payable is
  negative, so the floor never reads it as owed. The printed bill now names the table.
- **Floor.** All four bill reads (tiles, the tap, Clear, the idle clock) use `sittingId`. One domain
  constructor, `sittingOf`, builds every sitting and throws when a billed line's bill is missing.
- **Blank tiles.** A second bug on the same seam: staff logins live in `sessions` with status
  `active`, so every logged-in cashier, captain and kitchen tablet was drawn as a blank tile. The
  floor now skips `entity: 'server'`.
- **Clear** refuses a blank table id by name instead of a 500.
- **Lines** require the guest sitting; `|| ""` and `|| tableId` are gone, and the unreferenced
  `orders/createOrder.js` (the only door without a session) is deleted. Note for the till screen
  of order entry: `SPEC_OR` R3a.
- **Why no test saw it.** Billing unit fixtures used `s1` for the cashier, the sitting and the draft;
  every till and e2e spec seeded bills in the floor's shape. Fixtures now keep the cashier on `st1`.

Independent review (`.claude/skills/moonshot-review.md`, Opus subagent): **PASS WITH P1, no P0.** Fixed
before closing: a broken billed line now surfaces as a typed `failed-precondition` naming the table, line and
bill (`BrokenSitting`), not "An unexpected error occurred"; credit notes are kept off the floor's sitting so a
credited table's tap does not offer the note as a bill; the billing helper is `billSitting` so it no longer
shares a name with the floor's `sittingOf`. Left as is (P2, unreachable today): a line stamped with a staff
session would be dropped from the floor silently; checkout validation makes that impossible.
