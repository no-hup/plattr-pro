on the guest app. Kitchen and captain are done by API. The till bills it, takes cash and clears
the table. This time it was scoped to two apps on purpose: this is the first cross-app run of this
shape, and a clean result on two apps is worth more than a shaky one on five. One agent drove and
checked its own work. Trajectory:
[test/e2e/results/sanity-run-2-2026-09-25.jsonl](../../backend/src-plattr/functions/test/e2e/results/sanity-run-2-2026-09-25.jsonl).

## New findings

### S2-1 · P2 · A paid, cleared table leaves its order `IN_PROGRESS` for good

*Scene.* 20:05. Table 6 pays ₹309 cash and the cashier taps Clear. The till says "6 cleared" and
the table is free. But the order behind it is still open. The captain's active-order read still
returns it. When the next party sits at table 6 and orders a naan, table 6 now carries **two**
`IN_PROGRESS` orders: the paid one and the new one.

*Steps from a clean seed.* Re-import MockData7. Put table 6 through guest checkout, kitchen READY,
server SERVED, till issue, cash, Clear (or run `floorstate.mjs 6 settled` and then Clear from the till).
Then log in as `server@meg.test` and call `order-getActiveOrdersForRestaurant`.

*Expected.* Once the bill is paid and the table is cleared, the order is closed (`COMPLETED`, or
whatever state the spec names), and no active-order read returns it.

*Seen.* Till: "6 cleared", tile "6 free". Database: `orders/fo0yudPqEXDgUgCLO61v`
`orderStatus: IN_PROGRESS`, `paymentStatus: paid`; the table is `vacant` and the session `ended`.
`order-getActiveOrdersForRestaurant` returns it (0 carts once served carts are filtered). After
`floorstate.mjs 6 ordered`, table 6 has orders `UUGNagASR7CIUoIwO694` (new) and `fo0yud…` (old),
both `IN_PROGRESS`. The next party did get a fresh order, so grouping by table did not swallow it.

*Log.* `floor-clear`, `floor-get`. There is no error and no order write.

*Evidence.* Trajectory hops 6 and 7.

*Where to look.* Nothing on the till path writes `orderStatus`. Only `orders/updateOrderStatus.js`
(the captain's Mark Paid) closes an order, and the till has replaced that step. Close to TD-010
("two writers for one idea"), but that row is about `paymentStatus`, not the order never closing.

### Notes (not filed as bugs)

- **Why not table 1.** MockData7 seeds `tbl_meg_1` as occupied: session `ses_meg_active` for Customer
  One, plus a pre-filled shared cart (`carts/tbl_meg_1`: chicken biryani ×1, paneer 65 ×2, base ₹830).
  A guest scanning it gets the "ask Customer One for the OTP" join dialog and a cart they didn't fill.
  That breaks spine rule 1 (state from a seed), and the biryani and the ₹499+ total would fire both
  offers. The spine moved to vacant table 6.
- **On table 1, the joining guest can't clear the cart.** `cart-removeItemFromCart` answers 403
  "That item is not yours to remove." for Customer One's items. That is right, but no message was
  seen on screen, and the row's controls disappeared until a reload. Remove and − on the joiner's own
  Chicken 65 fired no call at all. This may be a click-dispatch issue of the tool (FRONTEND_TESTING
  §8, "some buttons ignore `.click()`"), so it is unconfirmed. That Chicken 65 was left in table 1's
  cart and never checked out. Worth one cell in a consumer exploration.
- The consumer's hash route dropped to `/?agent=1` after checkout, while the page still showed
  "Order placed successfully!".
- Guest session `createdAt` and `expiresAt` are stored as `{_seconds, _nanoseconds}` maps, not
  Timestamps. The seed's `ses_meg_active` uses real Timestamps. The two writers disagree. This is the
  known emulator-sentinel gotcha (memory), and not new.
- Issuing a bill writes a state log line with its `cid` but no audit row. Audit holds floor, approval
  and day-close acts only. If issue is meant to be an audited act, that is a gap; otherwise fine.
- The order's `cid` is the order id, while the bill's and payment's `cid` is `till_<sessionId>`. So
  two `cid`s cover one sitting.
- **Known, confirmed present:** TD-052 (re-logins, above); the raw `SERVICE_CHARGE 5%` label on the
  bill (bill-screen QA, P3); "Table bill" with no table number in the heading (TD-081 wording).
  `#A-0001` on the bill vs. "Bill 0001 issued" in the message: two spellings of one number on one
  screen, P3, likely covered by TD-072's wording group.
- **Environment noise:** 26 `getActiveCartsForKitchen: Invalid or inactive session` errors in the
  slot-0 log came from a client this run did not start. It was probably a kitchen tab left open
  elsewhere, polling `:5002` with a dead session. They were told apart by function name, as §3
  advises.

## Logs checked at every step

- **Emulator log:** filtered to function names and error/warn lines per step (quoted in each trajectory
  row). The only errors in the run's window were the expected 401 on the scan
  (`validateTableAndLocation` "Authentication required"), the table-1 403 above, and the stray kitchen
  poller.
- **Consumer app log:** `backend/flutter-app-logs/consumer.log` holds only build output under
  `-d web-server`. The app's runtime errors surface in the browser console only.
- **Browser console:** consumer has the known `setState() called during build` for `MenuState`
  (FRONTEND_TESTING §12), the 401 and the 403. The till console had no errors.

## What felt wrong from the cashier's chair

- 4 logins for one ₹309 bill, 5 if you count Clear (TD-052). This is the loudest thing on the till.
- After Generate, the screen has nowhere to go (S2-2).
- The guest's app says "Total Amount: ₹280.00" and the bill says ₹309.00. That is correct (charge and
  GST come at billing), but a guest who reads the phone will query the bill. A line like "+ service
  charge and GST at billing" would head that off.
- The payment screen shows "outstanding ₹309.00" without the table or bill number. With two bills
  open, the cashier can't see which one they are taking cash on.

## What this run proved about the method

- A two-app spine with the middle steps by API still crosses both seams that matter (guest to
  billing lines, bill to floor), and it caught one thing no single screen shows (S2-1). This was only
  visible by reading the order after Clear and asking the captain's read about it.
- The spine table's default table is wrong for MockData7. Table 1 is seeded occupied with a
  pre-filled cart. A spine needs a vacant table (6+).
