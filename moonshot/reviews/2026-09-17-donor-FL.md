- No rule refuses merging a table that is **already in another group**, or a group that would then hold two live bills; FL-S9 only covers "occupied". 21:20 Friday, the cashier merges table 6 (already 5+6, ₹4,120 open) into table 8 and one group's food lands on a bill nobody reads. `ury_order.py:63-105,237`

## DIFFERENT
- ury stores `occupied` on the table row on every save; the sheet derives every tile from live line snapshots each poll (R2). Ours is safer for the money — ury needed a whole reconcile pass precisely because the stored flag drifts. `ury_order.py:1585-1593` vs `:306-330`
- ury frees the table on bill **print**; the sheet holds `settled` until someone clears it (FL-S14). Ours is safer at 11pm: a printed-but-unpaid table reading free is a walkout nobody sees. `ury_order.py:385-412`
- Merge shape: ury walks a symmetric partner list, Odoo uses a parent pointer with a cycle check that rolls back; the sheet's `mergedInto` is a parent pointer with no chain or cycle guard named. Odoo's rollback is safer — a chained group has a total no lone cashier can compute. `pos_restaurant.py:140-150`
## OURS ALONE
- The tile carries money and elapsed minutes, and deliberately stops before service charge and round-off (R2, FL-S19); donors colour the tile occupied and nothing more. `dolibarr/floors.php:87-93`, `ury_order.py:1585-1593`
- Tile state routes the tap on payment state — `billed`/`settled` open tender, never a second bill — and the floor greys out when stale and refuses merge/unmerge/move offline (FL-S3, FL-S14, FL-S15). No donor floor knows about payment or about its own staleness. `dolibarr/floors.php:192-219`
