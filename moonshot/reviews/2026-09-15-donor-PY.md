# Donor inventory — payments, tenders, change, refunds, day close (PY)

Read before opening `SPEC_PY_payments.md`. Paths are relative to `~/Desktop/moonshot/donors/`.

## What a working POS does

### The tender row itself
- A payment is its own record, many per order, never a field on the order — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:15-23`, `pos_order.py:356`.
- The row carries method, amount, its own timestamp, the session, the cashier and the company — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:22-30`.
- It also carries what the acquirer said: card type, brand, last four, cardholder, reference number, approval code, issuer bank, transaction id, status, receipt text — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:31-41`.
- Change is a payment row of its own, flagged `is_change`, not a negative field — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:42`; SambaPOS keeps change in a second list entirely — `samba/Ticket.cs:38-39,261-267`.
- Every row has a client-generated uuid with a unique constraint, so an offline till replaying a sync cannot double-tender — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:44-46`.
- Money is `decimal`, rounded to a configured number of places — `samba/Ticket.cs:436-441`.

### Taking a bill to paid
- The server recomputes `amount_paid` from the rows and says so: "we don't trust the client" — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:194-195`.
- Paid means sum(rows) == total, checked server-side at the transition, and it raises — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:874-888`; SambaPOS refuses to close a ticket with a remainder — `samba/Ticket.cs:79-86`.
- Underpay on an order already marked paid raises; overpay only warns and is logged — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:616-622`. **WRONG:** overpay should not be a warning.
- Dolibarr clamps server-side instead: an amount ≤ 0 or above the remainder becomes exactly the remainder — `dolibarr/invoice.php:428-433`.
- The bill flips to paid only when the remainder reaches zero, and the payment method is stamped on it then — `dolibarr/invoice.php:464-473`.
- A paid order cannot be pushed back to draft or edited — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:609-611`.
- Once the receipt has printed, the payments are frozen — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:623-624`.
- The whole settle is one DB transaction, rolled back on any failure, with the in-memory object restored too — `dolibarr/invoice.php:342,540-546`.
- A failed settle is a hard throw with a named reason, never a silent no-op — `ury/ury/ury/doctype/ury_order/ury_order.py:2074-2077`, `dolibarr/invoice.php:441-456`.

### Several tenders on one bill
- Odoo: n rows summing to the total; there is no "split" verb, splitting *is* more rows — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:510-516`.
- URY takes a list of `{mode_of_payment, amount}` in one call, clears the server copy and rebuilds it — `ury/ury/ury/doctype/ury_order/ury_order.py:1997,2030,2067-2070`.
- On a merged bill URY spreads each tender across the two invoices capped at each one's rounded total, so no rupee is counted twice — `ury/ury/ury/doctype/ury_order/ury_order.py:2041-2055`.
- SambaPOS tracks *which lines* are already settled, so a table can be paid item by item across tenders — `samba/Ticket.cs:169-173,811-814`, and lines worth more than the unpaid remainder cannot be pulled — `samba/Ticket.cs:482-485`.
- Dolibarr and URY also split by *moving lines to a second bill*, not by amount — `dolibarr/split.php:66-138` (raw `UPDATE facturedet SET fk_facture`, `:97,128`; both totals recomputed `:131-137`), `ury/ury/ury/doctype/ury_order/ury_order.py:545-570`, new bill inherits the source's tender at its own rounded total `:649-658`.
- Splitting is refused on anything but a draft, cross-branch, or without write permission — `ury/ury/ury/doctype/ury_order/ury_order.py:552,563,566`.

### Change on cash
- Change needs a cash method on the session or the settle errors — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:197-200`.
- The change row is netted against the first cash tender before it hits the books, so the drawer shows one movement — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:74-93`.
- **WRONG:** the change figure itself is read straight off the client dict, unlike `amount_paid` two lines above — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:197-204`. Same in Dolibarr: `pos_change` is the browser's `excess` field, cash only — `dolibarr/invoice.php:424-426`.
- Rounding to the smallest note: only the cash residual is rounded, non-cash tenders pay their exact share — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:786-796`; tolerance at paid-time is half the rounding unit — `:889-898`; config at `pos_config.py:174-176`.
- **WRONG:** a `TODO` in code says mixed cash/non-cash with cash-only rounding is unsupported — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:877`.

### Voiding a payment row, refunds
- A payment cannot be edited once the order is posted — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:60-64`.
- Removing a tender removes its ledger transaction with it, never orphaning one — `samba/Ticket.cs:280-290,834-840`.
- Every add, change and removal of a tender is written to the order's own log, naming old and new method and amount — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:626-631,635-685`.
- A finalised bill is never edited: a refund is a **new reversing order** in the currently-open session, `amount_paid` reset to 0, its own bill number — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:1390-1403,1426-1451`.
- No open session, no refund — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:1436-1437`.
- Only lines not already refunded are pulled in, and only one original order per refund — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:1441`, `:1269-1277`.
- Dolibarr does the same as a credit note that must point at a real validated invoice for that customer, else it fails — `dolibarr/invoice.php:344-367,549-561`; the button only exists on a valid or closed bill — `:2038-2043`.
- Cancel applies to drafts only; a paid order raises "already been paid" — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:1239-1246`.

### Who may do it
- Reading the closing screen needs the POS-user group; cash in/out and deleting a cash move are two *separate* rights — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:779-780,1880-1881,1894-1899`.
- Deleting a cash move is logged against the person who did it — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:1900-1904`.
- A payment method cannot be edited at all while a session using it is open — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment_method.py:139-153`.
- A tender must be one the session's config allows — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:66-70`.
- One cash method may not be shared by two shops — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment_method.py:220-228`.
- **WRONG:** Dolibarr gates the entire payment action on one blanket `takepos/run` right — `dolibarr/invoice.php:312`.
- URY: the main cashier cannot close the day while a sub-cashier's till is open, and a sub-cashier cannot make the day-close entry — `ury/ury/ury/hooks/ury_pos_closing_entry.py:11-33,61-70`.

### Method definition
- Method is config, not code: name, order, journal, accounts, "identify customer", active flag, image — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment_method.py:23-60`.
- Cash / bank / customer-account is derived from the journal, not typed by hand — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment_method.py:59,115-121`.
- Customer-account ("pay later") tenders settle nothing at the till and are excluded from the drawer — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:83-84`, `pos_session.py:783`; Dolibarr's "delayed" writes no payment, only terms — `dolibarr/invoice.php:459-461`.
- UPI/QR is a method type with a validated bank account and QR format — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment_method.py:61-68,202-212`, and a blank QR is precomputed so it still works offline — `:230-240`.
- A card terminal is an interface with start / status / cancel, and cancel is explicitly advisory because the terminal may already have captured — `ury/ury/ury/api/payment_terminal.py:49-71`.
- With no terminal configured the default refuses honestly rather than faking success — `ury/ury/ury/api/payment_terminal.py:74-89`; the simulator exists but is never registered by default — `:92-99,153-158`.
- Terminal attempts are their own record: transaction id, amount, currency, status, time — `ury/ury/ury/doctype/ury_payment_terminal_transaction/ury_payment_terminal_transaction.json`.

### What day close reads
- Expected cash = opening float + cash tenders + every cash in/out on the session — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:799-808`.
- Every non-cash method is reported with both its amount *and its count* — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:809-815`.
- Every cash in/out is listed with reason, amount, time and cashier name — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:756-776`.
- Counted cash is stored, and the difference posted against the journal's profit or loss account per method — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:654-678,680-716`.
- A difference above a configured amount blocks close for non-managers — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_config.py:121,166-169`, surfaced at `pos_session.py:816-817`.
- Draft orders block the close, and a second closer is told their sales went to a rescue session — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:391-394,718-737`.
- Non-cash methods can also be adjusted at close, but only if the journal has profit and loss accounts — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:738-754`.
- When the posted totals do not balance, close is refused and routed through a manager wizard, not forced — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:578-595`.
- URY's close sheet is per method: opening, expected, closing, difference — and expected/difference are permission-level 1, so the counting cashier cannot see the target — `ury/ury/ury/doctype/sub_pos_closing_payment/sub_pos_closing_payment.json:12-48`.
- URY's till total is the submitted, Paid invoices for *that cashier* in the window; drafts block — `ury/ury/ury/doctype/sub_pos_closing/sub_pos_closing.py:19-25,37-57`; sub-till and main till are summed per method — `ury/ury/ury/hooks/ury_pos_closing_entry.py:37-58`.
- The day's takings by method come off the payment rows, not the orders — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/report_sale_details.py:121-134,376-421`, `ury/ury/api/dashboard.py:91-94`; cash in/out appears in the same list as a pseudo-tender — `report_sale_details.py:274`.

### What the guest sees, what the kitchen sees
- The receipt carries the acquirer's own slip text and the card's last four — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:33,41`; the receipt can be emailed after the fact — `pos_order.py:1405-1424`.
- Recording a payment regenerates the bill PDF — `dolibarr/invoice.php:441`.
- An invoice can be raised after the sale and is reconciled against the tenders already taken — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:1173-1190,1235-1237`.
- **Nothing.** In all five donors the kitchen never learns a bill was paid — no payment path touches a preparation record.

## MISSING
- **A tab.** Donors have a third tender kind that settles nothing at the till and stays out of the drawer — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:83-84`, excluded from the close at `pos_session.py:783`, Dolibarr's "delayed" writes no payment at all and sets terms instead, `dolibarr/invoice.php:459-461`. R10 has only `cash | external`, and every take reduces outstanding. The regular who says "put it on my tab" at 22:30 for ₹1,840 leaves the cashier two choices: record cash that never entered the drawer, or leave the bill `issued` forever and have it block tomorrow's close.
- **Cash out of the drawer mid-shift.** Every cash movement that is not a sale is its own row with reason, amount, time and cashier — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:756-776`, written under its own right `:1879-1885`, deleted under a *second* right and logged `:1894-1904`. Out of scope names only "float and blind counts (DC)". The vegetable vendor is paid ₹1,200 from the drawer at 18:40; at 23:30 PY-S18 reports cash ₹12,450 and the drawer holds ₹11,250, and nothing anywhere says why.
- **Unpaid bills block the close.** Donors refuse to close the day while any bill is still a draft — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_session.py:391-394` and `:725-726`, `ury/ury/ury/doctype/sub_pos_closing/sub_pos_closing.py:19-25`; a second closer is told their sales went to a rescue session, `pos_session.py:727-737`. Talks-to has DC → PY for "is this date closed" but no arrow back. Table 7's ₹2,400 bill is still `issued` at 23:40, the owner closes, cash reconciles to the paisa, and ₹2,400 quietly becomes tomorrow's.
- **Who took it.** Donors split the day by cashier and will not let the main till close over an open sub-till — `ury/ury/ury/hooks/ury_pos_closing_entry.py:11-33`, per-cashier takings at `ury/ury/ury/doctype/sub_pos_closing/sub_pos_closing.py:37-57`. `by` is on the row but R19 groups by `tenderId` only, and PY-Q2 already assumes two screens share one drawer. Cash is ₹800 short at 23:30 across two cashiers and there is no number that separates them.
- **The acquirer's own identifiers.** Donors keep card brand, last four, cardholder, approval code, issuer bank, acquirer transaction id and the slip text as their own fields — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:31-41`. Ours is one free-string `ref`. A ₹3,240 card row from 22:40 is charged back three weeks later; with one string and no approval code, nobody can tell the bank which tap it was.
- **A tender may not be changed while the day is open.** Donors forbid editing a payment method while any session using it is open, whitelisting only display order — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment_method.py:139-153`, and refuse a tender the shop's config does not allow, `pos_payment.py:66-70`. PY-S19 adds one mid-service and says nothing about removing one. The owner deletes `card` at 21:00 with a ₹400-part-paid bill open; the remaining ₹209 can no longer be taken on the tender the first half went out on.

## DIFFERENT
- Overcharge on a terminal in hand: Odoo warns and logs it (`odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:621-622`), Dolibarr silently clamps the charge down to the remainder (`dolibarr/invoice.php:428-433`). We refuse (PY-S5). Ours is safer for the money — a clamp books ₹609 while the machine charged ₹700 and nothing on the bill says the guest is ₹91 down.
- Change: Odoo takes the figure from the client (`odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:197-204`) two lines after saying it does not trust the client for `amount_paid` (`:194-195`); Dolibarr takes the browser's `excess` (`dolibarr/invoice.php:425`); URY computes the whole split, shortfall and change in the browser with `parseFloat` (`ury/pos/src/components/PaymentDialog.tsx:91-99,250-255`). We compute it server-side from the re-read outstanding (R8, PY-S33). Ours is safer for the money, without argument.
- Splitting: donors mostly move *lines* to a second bill (`dolibarr/split.php:66-138`, `ury/ury/ury/doctype/ury_order/ury_order.py:545-570,649-658`); only Odoo and SambaPOS split by amount (`odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:510-516`, `samba/Ticket.cs:169-173,811-814`). We split by amount and push lines to BL-S12. Ours is safer for the money — one bill number, one payable, nothing re-taxed — but theirs is what the lone cashier at 11pm is actually asked for when two guests each want their own invoice.
- Refund: donors let the POS itself raise the reversing document into the open session (`odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:1426-1451`, `dolibarr/invoice.php:549-561`). We require BL to issue the credit note first, and PY-S25 makes that impossible until the bill is fully collected. Ours is safer for the money — no cash leaves without a tax document behind it — but at 11pm it means "finish collecting from the guest you just admitted you overcharged", which theirs does not.
- A settled row: Odoo freezes payments once the receipt prints (`odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:623-624`) and forbids editing one on a posted order outright (`pos_payment.py:60-64`); the only correction is a refund. We let a settled row be voided with a PIN and reopen the bill (PY-S27). Theirs is safer for the money in the abstract — a voidable settled ticket is a way to make ₹609 vanish from a day — but R15's "no void after DC closed that date" (PY-S29) is the control that actually matters, and ours saves the 11pm cashier who tapped Cash instead of Card from a two-document dance.
- Idempotency: Odoo gets it from a unique uuid column and nothing else (`odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:44-46`) — a replay is a database error, not a success. We re-read and return the existing row (R13, PY-S24). Ours is safer for the lone cashier: an error on a retry is what makes someone void a real row and take the money twice.

## OURS ALONE
- `captured`: the same overshoot is refused when the terminal is still in hand and recorded as `overpaid` when the bank already has it (PY-S5 vs PY-S28, R6). No donor distinguishes them — Odoo warns on both (`odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:621-622`), Dolibarr clamps both (`dolibarr/invoice.php:428-433`).
- Status derived both ways on every write, so a void or refund downgrades a settled bill (R2, PY-S27, PY-S30). Every donor latches: `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py:609-611`, `dolibarr/invoice.php:464-471`.
- `businessDate` frozen on the row and shifted by the configured close time (R14, R18). Donors group by session id or a posting-date range — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:28`, `ury/ury/ury/doctype/sub_pos_closing/sub_pos_closing.py:42`.
- A retry carrying a known `paymentId` with a *different* amount is refused rather than overwritten. Nothing in `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:44-46` compares the amount behind the id.
- The whole tender row snapshotted onto the payment, `opensDrawer` and `needsRef` included. Donors store the method id and read the method live — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:23`.
- A cap on live tenders per bill (PY-S22). No donor caps them; Odoo's only guard is the paid-total check at `pos_order.py:874-888`.
- A refund of an overpay that names the payment row it returns and leaves `paidTotal` untouched (PY-S32, `refundsPaymentId`). Odoo would push it through a whole reversing order — `pos_order.py:1426-1451`.
- A void refused because day close has signed off that date (R15, PY-S29). Donors tie removal to document state only — `odoo/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_payment.py:60-64`, `samba/Ticket.cs:280-284`.
